# Pitch

A top-down action adventure in the spirit of the original Legend of Zelda, where you play a TV-headed robot living in a hopeful post-apocalyptic virtual world. Ruins are being reclaimed by nature. Human players' avatars, people and animals, spawn in at glowing pads, stay a while and log off; the robots live there permanently and perceive these visitors as spirits.

## Status at the pitch date

Hobby project, concept and placeholder-art stage. No engine chosen, no playable build yet.

Since then the game has been built on [bones](https://github.com/an-dr/bones) and is playable; see the [repository README](../../README.md) for the current state.
