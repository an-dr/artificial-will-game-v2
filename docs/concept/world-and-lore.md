# World and lore

The whole game takes place inside a virtual world that looks like a positive post-apocalypse: ruins and nature, not wastelands. Fantasy-flavoured areas are fine for the first levels.

- **Avatars:** people and animals are virtual avatars of human players. They appear at spawn spots, spend some time in the world (Sword Art Online style) and disappear.
- **Robots:** they feel the world is real and are its permanent residents. They perceive the avatars as spirits.
- **Two perceptions:** avatars see each other as normal people and animals; robots see the same visitors as translucent cyan spirits. Robots look the same in both views.

## Open question

The player is a real person playing a robot who sees people as spirits. Is that only world flavour, or a story twist (does the hero ever learn what the spirits are; can the player's own avatar appear as a spirit)? The answer decides whether "two perceptions" is art direction or a mechanic.
