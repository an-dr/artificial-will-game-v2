# CLAUDE.md

**Before every task:** Read the base agents policy
([an-dr/agents](https://github.com/an-dr/agents), installed globally) and
`AGENTS.md`. Open your response by stating the active flow and phase — or that
no flow applies (pure question/analysis) — and why.

The base policy is the instruction set; the root `AGENTS.md` extends it with
repo-specific context.
