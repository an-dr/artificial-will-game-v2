# level-three

The White Harbour coastal level for Artificial Will, built only from the
in-house placeholder pack under `assets/robot_game_assets/`. It publishes an
embedded sand, promenade, and sea map, places the harbour props (white houses,
lighthouse, trees, pier, boat, flower pots, rubble) and an animated spawn pad
with its light beam, and populates the shore with characters.

The level has no enemies and no combat. The four robot residents (Scrap,
Radio, Merchant, Gardener) stand still with colliders. The visiting spirits
(elder, child, fox, deer, partridge, hare) are pass-through: Will walks
through them, as through ghosts. Every character loops its idle animation.

Invisible fixed bodies block the open sea and the map's land edges. The pier
and boat are scenery only.
